# Dataset redistribution notice

`secom_telemetry_modified.csv` is the challenge-provided modified dataset used by the notebook. The package does not establish that the submitter has redistribution rights. Before pushing this CSV to a public repository, check the organizer's license and submission terms. If permission is absent or ambiguous, remove the CSV from the public repository and ZIP, tell reviewers how to obtain the official dataset, and keep a local copy beside `wafer_guard_fixed.ipynb` when executing it. The evaluator-only mask is not needed and must not be included.
